# North Star — what Twelve Words is

**Twelve Words** is the parent brand over a suite of bitcoin-native personal-finance apps.
The name is the mechanism: the twelve words of one Recovery Key sign into every app in
the suite — one key, two ledgers, more to come.

A small independent studio. Founder is pseudonymous (`silentius-satoshi`), $0 capital,
10–15 hrs/week, build-in-public posture.

## The thesis, as the site says it

> **One key. Every ledger.**
>
> Twelve Words builds personal-finance apps for people who hold bitcoin. The twelve words
> of one Recovery Key sign into all of them — and everything you record stays on your
> device.

## What the apex is for

Three jobs, and nothing else:

1. **Legibility** — explain the one-key suite thesis in a single screen, so a visitor
   understands what the company is before they understand any product.
2. **Voice** — carry the company's writing on its own domain, so the archive is owned
   rather than rented.
3. **Shared property** — hold the domain, email, brand system, and the disclaimer that
   every surface inherits.

It is deliberately minimal. Adding a section is a decision, not a default.

## How the suite works (the three claims the site makes)

- **One Recovery Key.** Twelve words, generated once, written on paper. The same key
  signs into every app. No password to reset, no account to recover — the key *is* the
  account.
- **Apps that never hold your data.** Records stay on the user's device. With sync on,
  entries move between their devices encrypted; we can't read them and can't lose them.
- **If we vanish, your data doesn't.** The code is public and the data stays with the
  user. If the company disappeared, their books, plans, and key keep working.

These are load-bearing promises. Never write copy that overstates them, and never ship
architecture that makes any of them untrue.
